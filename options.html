A reference, not a recommendation. This page explains what each structure does and which view it expresses. It says nothing about any particular stock, and nothing on it is investment advice. Every strike and premium is a round illustrative figure on a $100 stock, picked so the eleven shapes can be compared side by side — they are not quotes.
