# GE Vernova Q3 FY26

**Summary**: GE Vernova's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $12,642M of revenue, up 27% year over year, a 25% gross margin, $1,394M of operating income and $1,289M of net income. Orders of $29.3 billion grew 96% organically and backlog reached $197 billion. This is GE Vernova's first card on the Financials tab.

**Sources**: GE Vernova 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed October 28, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000163/gev-20260930.htm), and 8-K exhibit 99.1 of the same date for the segment and orders figures. Debt, cash and free cash flow from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-10-28.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

## Why the statement comes from the 10-Q

GE Vernova's earnings release is built around **adjusted EBITDA**. It carries segment revenue,
segment cost of revenues, SG&A and R&D, orders and backlog — but no consolidated profit and loss
statement. The 10-Q filed the same morning carries one, and that is what the chart draws. This is
the first card in the repo sourced from a 10-Q rather than an 8-K exhibit.

## Income statement

Three months ended September 30, in millions of USD.

| Line | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenues | 12,642 | 9,969 | +27% |
| Cost of equipment and services | 9,536 | 8,071 | +18% |
| **Gross profit** | **3,107** | **1,897** | **+64%** |
| Selling, general and administrative | 1,354 | 1,221 | +11% |
| Research and development | 358 | 310 | +15% |
| **Operating income** | **1,394** | **366** | **+281%** |
| Interest and other financial income, net | 71 | 44 | +61% |
| Non-operating benefit income | 212 | 115 | +84% |
| Other income, net | 105 | 221 | −52% |
| Provision for income taxes | 493 | 293 | +68% |
| **Net income** | **1,289** | **453** | **+185%** |
| Net income attributable to GE Vernova | 1,291 | 452 | +186% |

Gross margin 24.6%, up from 19.0%. Operating margin 11.0%, up from 3.7%. Net margin 10.2%.
Diluted EPS $4.81 against $1.64, up 193%, on 268M diluted shares.

**The margin, not the revenue, is the quarter.** Revenue grew 27% and gross profit grew 64%,
because cost of equipment and services grew only 18%. GE Vernova attributes that to price, volume
and productivity. Operating income almost quadrupled off a small base.

The segments are reported inclusive of intersegment sales and sum to $12,670M against $12,642M of
consolidated revenue. The $29M difference is intersegment eliminations, so on the Financials card
the three segment bands are 0.2% wider in total than the revenue bar they feed — inside the 0.5%
guard the chart builder applies, and said on the card.

**Electrification's 51% is mostly bought.** Organic Electrification revenue is $2,914M, 16%
growth; the rest is the Prolec GE acquisition, in which GE Vernova bought out the remaining 50%
stake from Xignux. Power's 25% is organic, led by Gas Power equipment. Within Power, Gas Power is
$5,117M, Nuclear Power $719M and Hydro Power $231M.

Wind grew 4% and still earns a 2.6% segment EBITDA margin — positive, where it was −2.3% a year
ago, and loss-making for the nine months at −9.4%.

## Backlog is the forward number

Orders were **$29.3 billion**, up 96% organically, and backlog grew $20 billion sequentially to
$197 billion. Gas Power equipment backlog plus slot reservation agreements grew from 116 GW to
122 GW, and GE Vernova expects at least 130 GW by the end of 2026. Electrification equipment
backlog is $42.2 billion, up 60% year over year.

## Balance sheet

$2,795M of debt against $16,764M of cash — **net cash of $13,969M**, five times gross
debt — and $5,410M of free cash flow in the quarter. The company raised 2026 guidance to revenue
of $46.5-$47.5 billion, adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.5%-14.5% and free cash flow of $16-$17
billion, the last up from $11.5-$12.5 billion.

In October the board raised the share repurchase authorisation to $20 billion from $10 billion,
and GE Vernova agreed to buy Trench Group, a high-voltage grid equipment maker, for about $9.0
billion, closing late 2027.

## A caution on the nine-month figures

Year to date GE Vernova shows $6,687M of net income on $33,084M of revenue, a 20% net margin. That
is not the operating business: the nine months include a **$3,992M pre-tax gain** on remeasuring
the previously held Prolec GE stake to fair value and a $366M gain on the sale of the Proficy
business. Neither is in Q3. The quarter's own other income is $105M.
