# CoreWeave Q2 FY26

**Summary**: CoreWeave's Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026): $2,575M revenue, up 112% year over year, at a 66% gross margin, but a $49M operating loss and a $626M net loss — the gap between the two is almost entirely financing cost.

**Sources**: CoreWeave Q2 2026 results release, investor relations (source: investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Reports-Strong-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/). Revenue growth cross-checked against stockanalysis.com. Backlog and guidance figures come from the release and third-party summaries of it.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-22

Cash covers 11% of debt. The quarter's free cash outflow of $5.7B is **larger than the entire cash

## Where the loss comes from

The operating loss is small — $49M on $2.6B of revenue. The net loss is **thirteen times larger**, and the difference is the capital structure:

- Net interest expense was **$640M**, up 2.4× from $267M a year earlier
- Other income of $125M offset part of it, so interest and other netted to **$515M**
- Tax added a further $62M expense despite the pre-tax loss

This is a debt-financed buildout showing up in the income statement. Adjusted EBITDA was $1,510M (59% margin) and adjusted operating income $128M, so the distance between the non-GAAP and GAAP pictures is depreciation plus interest.

Technology and infrastructure spending of $1,507M is 59% of revenue, which is what converts a 66% gross margin into an operating loss.

## Scale and guidance

Revenue backlog was about **$104B** at June 30, with more than $25B of net new commitments added in early Q3. Full-year 2026 guidance is $12.4–13.2B of revenue against **$35–39B of capex** — capex roughly three times revenue.
