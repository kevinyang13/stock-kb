# Coinbase Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Coinbase's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $1,215.6M of total revenue, down 35% year over year, with operating income of $61.7M — a 5% operating margin — and net income of $718.7M. The gap between those two is almost entirely a $715.0M mark-to-market gain on crypto Coinbase holds for its own account.

**Sources**: Coinbase 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed October 29, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978826000122), and the Q3 2026 earnings presentation filed the same day as 8-K exhibit 99.1.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-29

## The 59% net margin is a balance sheet, not a business

Operating income was **$61.7M on $1,215.6M of revenue** — a 5% operating margin. Net income was
**$718.7M**. The difference is $762M of non-operating income, of which **$715.0M is a gain on crypto
assets held for investment**: a mark on Coinbase's own holdings, not anything a customer paid it.

Strip it out and the quarter is roughly break-even. A net margin built on a price move describes
the crypto market, not the exchange. The same distortion, from the other direction, produced
[[iren-fq4-fy26]]'s impairment-driven loss.

## Revenue fell because trading fell

| Source | Q3 FY26 | share | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction revenue | 590,224 | 49% | −44% |
| Subscription & services | 570,238 | 47% | −20% |
| Other revenue | 55,120 | 5% | −48% |

Coinbase attributes the transaction fall to a **59% decline in consumer spot trading volume** and a
53% decline in institutional spot volume, partly offset by prediction markets and derivatives.
Monthly transacting users were 7.4 million against 9.3 million.

Subscription & services is now 47% of revenue against 38% a year ago. It did not grow — it fell
20%. Transaction revenue simply fell faster. Within it, stablecoin revenue was $306.9M (−5%) and
blockchain rewards $85.7M (−54%).

Assets on platform were **$376.6B against $515.9B**, which the company attributes mostly to crypto
prices rather than outflows.

## A note on how the card draws this

Coinbase has no conventional cost of revenue. The Financials card uses transaction expense ($194M)
as the cost line, which is the convention the previous Coinbase cards used. SG&A on the card is
sales and marketing plus general and administrative ($561M) **net of $49M of operating credits** —
the crypto gains on operational holdings, a restructuring reversal and other operating income —
because those are reported inside operating expenses and a Sankey cannot draw a negative band.
