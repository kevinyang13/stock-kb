# Constellation Energy Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Constellation Energy's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $9,550M of revenue, up 45% year over year, $2,492M of operating income and $1,835M of net income. The headline growth rate is misleading — two thirds of the increase is revenue from outside contracts with customers, which more than tripled. This is the first CEG card on the Financials tab.

**Sources**: Constellation Energy 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed November 9, 2026 (source: sec.gov, accession 0001868275-26-000119) — consolidated statements of operations (R8) and the disaggregation of revenue note (R55). Balance sheet and cash flow figures from stockanalysis.com quarterly statements, column 2026-09-30. Market figures from stockanalysis.com, November 9, 2026.

## This card follows the filing, not the aggregator

stockanalysis reports **$2,480M** of operating income for this quarter. The 10-Q reports
**$2,492M**.

The difference is the **$12M gain on sales of assets**, which the filing presents inside the
operating result — on its own line between total operating expenses and operating income — and
the vendor does not. It is a small gap in dollars and a real one in principle: the same check
caught a $269M gap on [[vistra-q3-fy26]], filed the same day, and three larger ones a week
earlier on [[iren-fq1-fy27]], [[cloudflare-q3-fy26]] and [[mda-space-q3-fy26]].

The vendor also reports prior-year total operating expenses of $5,497M against the filing's
$5,484M, a $13M difference in the taxes-other-than-income line.

## No gross profit line, so the chart builds one

Utilities do not report gross profit, and CEG does not. The chart treats **purchased power and
fuel** as cost of revenue and leaves operating and maintenance, depreciation and taxes other
than income in operating expenses:

| | |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $9,550M |
| Cost of revenue (purchased power and fuel) | $4,400M |
| **Gross profit (derived)** | **$5,150M — 54%** |

That 54% is a construction of this card. **It is not comparable with the other cards on the
Financials tab**, where gross profit is either filed or derived from a filed subtotal. The same
caveat applies to [[vistra-q3-fy26]] and, for a different reason, to [[ast-spacemobile-q3-fy26]].

One further compromise is on the card's face: the chart cannot draw a negative charge, so the
$12M asset-sale gain is netted against the $225M of taxes other than income, drawn as a single
$213M band. The operating result is the filed $2,492M either way.

## Read the 45% carefully

The disaggregation note splits revenue a second way, between revenue from contracts with
customers and everything else:

| | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenues from contracts with customers | $6,776M | $5,703M | **+19%** |
| **Other revenue sources** | **$2,774M** | **$867M** | **+220%** |
| **Total** | **$9,550M** | **$6,570M** | +45% |

**Of the $2,980M increase in revenue, $1,907M — 64% of it — is the increase in revenue from
outside contracts with customers.** That line is now 29% of the company, up from 13%.

Revenue from actually selling power to customers under contract grew 19%. The 45% headline is
mostly the other thing. This is the same caution the Financials tab applies wherever a mark or a
non-operating item is larger than the operating story — see [[iren-fq1-fy27]] and
[[alphabet-q3-fy26]].

**Capacity revenues nearly quintupled**, from $104M to $496M. PJM capacity auctions clear at
prices set years ahead and move in steps, so a near-fivefold increase describes an auction
outcome rather than a trend.

## Balance sheet

**$23,387M of debt against $603M of cash** at September 30, 2026 — net debt of **$22,784M**.
Free cash flow for the quarter was **+$2,486M**.

Net debt is twelve times the quarter's operating income and over two years of free cash flow at
this rate. For a regulated-adjacent generator that is an ordinary capital structure, not a
distress signal, but it is a different shape from everything else on this tab.
