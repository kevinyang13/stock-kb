# Vertiv Holdings Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Vertiv's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $4,312.8M of net sales, up 61% year over year, a 38% gross margin, $983.8M of operating profit and $764.5M of net income. The quarter beat Vertiv's own guidance by $463M and organic orders grew more than 100%.

**Sources**: Vertiv Holdings 8-K exhibit 99.1, filed October 28, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674101/000162828026067707/q32026exhibit991vrt10282026.htm). Debt, cash and free cash flow from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-10-28. Supersedes [[vertiv-q2-fy26]].

## It beat its own guidance by $463M

With the Q2 release Vertiv guided Q3 net sales of **$3,650M to $3,850M**. It delivered $4,312.8M —
$463M above the top of that range, and 15% above the midpoint. Full-year guidance went up to
$14,450M-$14,850M from $13,800M-$14,200M, a $650M raise at the midpoint.

Organic orders grew **more than 100%** year over year, which is the figure Vertiv led its release
with and the one that sets up Q4 and 2027.

## Segments

Vertiv's three reportable segments are geographic and foot to net sales exactly.

| Segment | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 | Δ | Δ% | Organic Δ% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Americas | 2,988.8 | 1,712.4 | +1,276.4 | +74.5% | +66.2% |
| Asia Pacific | 779.3 | 519.8 | +259.5 | +49.9% | +48.3% |
| Europe, Middle East & Africa | 544.7 | 443.6 | +101.1 | +22.8% | +19.3% |
| **Total** | **4,312.8** | **2,675.8** | **+1,637.0** | **+61.2%** | **+55.0%** |

**Growth is broad now, which it was not in Q2.** Last quarter EMEA grew 1.7% while the Americas
grew 29%, and the page on that quarter said the quarter *was* the Americas. This quarter EMEA grew
22.8% and Asia Pacific 49.9%. The Americas still contributed 78% of the increase, but every region
is growing double digits organically — EMEA was down 3.3% organically over the nine months and is
up 19.3% in the quarter.

Of the 61.2% total, 55.0% is organic and about 6% came from acquisitions.

## Balance sheet

$3,268.2M of debt against $3,512.9M of cash — **Vertiv is now net cash by $244.7M**, having
carried $527.6M of net debt at June 30. Free cash flow was $654.9M in the quarter (Vertiv's own
adjusted free cash flow measure is $652M). Operating cash flow was $794M.
