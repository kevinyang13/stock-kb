# Vertiv Holdings Q2 FY26

**Summary**: Vertiv's second quarter 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026): $3,274.3M of net sales, up 24% year over year, a 38% gross margin, $637.9M of operating profit and $497.8M of net income. Growth is an Americas story — the Americas segment grew 29% and EMEA grew 1.7%. Q3 2026 has since been reported and is charted on [[vertiv-q3-fy26]]; this page is kept as the record of the quarter before it.

## Segments

Vertiv's three reportable segments are geographic, and they foot to net sales exactly.

| Segment | Q2 FY26 | Q2 FY25 | Δ | Δ% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Americas | 2,070.8 | 1,602.3 | +468.5 | +29.2% |
| Asia Pacific | 719.9 | 560.2 | +159.7 | +28.5% |
| Europe, Middle East & Africa | 483.6 | 475.6 | +8.0 | +1.7% |
| **Total** | **3,274.3** | **2,638.1** | **+636.2** | **+24.1%** |

**The quarter is the Americas.** It is 63% of sales and contributed $468.5M of the $636.2M of
growth. EMEA added $8.0M on a base of $475.6M, and over the six months EMEA is actually **down**
11.7% in products. Asia Pacific grew 28.5% with no acquisition contribution at all.

Acquisitions did part of the Americas' work: of its $468.5M increase, $124.1M came from
acquisitions and $6.4M was lost to FX, leaving **$338.0M of organic growth, 21.1%**. Asia Pacific's
organic growth was 25.7% — the fastest in the company once FX is taken out.

## A split that disagrees with itself

The income statement and the segment table disaggregate the same $3,274.3M differently:

| Source | Products | Services |
|---|---|---|
| Statement of operations | 2,646.7 | 627.6 (services) |
| Sales by product and service offering | 2,606.4 | 667.9 (services **& spares**) |

Both foot to $3,274.3M, and the gap is $40.3M of spares that the segment table groups with services
and the income statement groups with products. Neither is wrong; they are different cuts. The
Financials card quotes the segment-table pair and says "services and spares" so the label matches
the figure. This is the same class of trap as the cost-of-revenue-against-gross-profit mismatches
recorded in [[sankey-income-statement]].

## Balance sheet

$3,338.2M of debt against $2,810.6M of cash, so net debt of $527.6M, with $926.5M of free cash flow
in the quarter — Vertiv could clear its gross debt inside four quarters of free cash flow. Cash
rose from $1,728.4M at December 31, 2025 to $2,810.6M at June 30, 2026.

Vertiv pays a token dividend: $0.25 a year, a 0.12% yield.

## What comes next
