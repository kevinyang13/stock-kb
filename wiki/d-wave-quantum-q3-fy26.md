# D-Wave Quantum Q3 FY26

**Summary**: D-Wave's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $4.5M of revenue, up 21%, at a gross margin that fell 18 points to 52.9%. The operating loss nearly doubled to $53.5M. The net loss improved to $48.2M only because last year's quarter carried a $121.9M warrant charge.

**Sources**: D-Wave Quantum Form 8-K exhibit 99.1, results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed November 10, 2026 (source: sec.gov, accession 0001907982-26-000180) — condensed consolidated statements of operations and balance sheets. Debt and cash-flow figures from stockanalysis.com, column 2026-09-30. Market figures from stockanalysis.com, November 10, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-10

## The improvement in the net loss is the absence of a warrant charge

The release leads with the net loss narrowing by $91.8M. Almost all of it is one line that is
no longer there: the year-ago quarter carried a **$121.9M non-cash charge** on remeasuring
warrant liabilities, and the remaining public warrants were redeemed in November 2025, so the
line is now zero and will stay zero.

Strip it out and the direction reverses. The **operating loss nearly doubled**, from $27.7M to
$53.5M, on operating expenses up 84%. R&D alone more than doubled.

This is the same reading problem as [[rigetti-q3-fy26]], filed the same day: for both names
the net line is dominated by warrant accounting and the operating line is the one that
describes the business.

## Gross margin fell 18 points on mix

Gross margin was **52.9%**, against 71.4% a year ago. The company attributes it to a revenue
mix weighted toward professional services, which carry a lower margin, plus $0.2M more
stock-based compensation inside cost of revenue. Non-GAAP gross margin, which strips
depreciation and stock compensation out of cost of revenue, was 63.6% against 77.7%.

The card uses the **GAAP** figure. Where a company offers both, this wiki charts the filed one
— the same rule that makes the [[favorites-financials]] note on HIVE's depreciation treatment a
recorded contradiction rather than a choice.

## The tax line is a benefit, folded into the non-operating bar

Income tax was a **$68K benefit**, not a charge. The loss layout cannot draw a negative cost,
so the benefit is folded into the non-operating source alongside the $5,217K of other income,
and the chart's green band reads $5.2M. The same treatment is used for IonQ's tax benefit —
see [[favorites-financials]].

## No segment split on this card, and the reason is timing

The Q2 FY26 card carried a three-way split — QCaaS, professional services, system sales — taken
from that quarter's 10-Q. **This card has none, because the 10-Q has not been filed.**

The press release gives *changes*, not levels: professional services +$1.5M, QCaaS +$0.8M,
system sales −$1.5M. Turning those into this quarter's split would need the prior-year levels,
which are not in the release, and deriving segment values from rounded deltas is not something
this wiki does. The split goes on the card when the 10-Q lands.

## Bookings, RPO and the CHIPS agreement

- **Bookings $1.9M** in the quarter, *down* 19% year over year. Nine-month bookings were
  **$37.4M**, up 611% — the quarter and the year point in opposite directions, and the company
  notes that single-quarter bookings vary with the timing of large transactions.
- **Remaining performance obligations $38.3M** at September 30, against $2.9M a year earlier.
  59% is expected to convert to revenue within 12 months, 71% within 24.
- Cash and marketable investment securities **$503.4M** ($279.4M of cash and equivalents plus
  $224.0M of marketable securities).
- A **definitive agreement with the US Department of Commerce** for access to up to $100M of
  CHIPS and Science Act funding, released against milestones. None of it is in these figures.

Nine-month revenue being down 52% while nine-month bookings are up 611% is the tension in this
quarter: what has been sold is not yet what has been recognised.
