# IREN FQ1 FY27

**Summary**: IREN's fiscal Q1 2027 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $228M of revenue, down 5% year over year, and a $263M net loss. The headline number barely moved; underneath it the company swapped businesses. AI Cloud went from $7M to $200M in a year while Bitcoin Mining fell from $233M to $28M.

**Sources**: IREN 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed November 5, 2026 (source: sec.gov, accession 0001878848-26-000066) — statement of operations (R4) and disaggregation of revenue note (R55). Balance sheet and cash flow figures from stockanalysis.com quarterly statements, column 2026-09-30. Market figures from stockanalysis.com, November 5, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-05

## The business changed underneath a flat revenue line

Total revenue fell 5%. That is the least interesting fact about this quarter.

| | FQ1 FY27 | FQ4 FY26 | FQ1 FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Cloud | **$200M** | $70.5M | $7M |
| Bitcoin Mining | **$28M** | $66.7M | $233M |
| AI Cloud share of revenue | **88%** | 51% | 3% |

[[iren-fq4-fy26]] recorded the crossover quarter, the first in which AI Cloud was the larger of
the two. One quarter later it is not a crossover but a replacement: mining is 12% of the
business and falling, AI Cloud nearly tripled sequentially.

Inside AI Cloud the 10-Q splits further: **AI Cloud Services $135M** and **AI Cloud Lease $65M**,
the lease being $49M of fixed and $16M of variable income. The lease line did not exist a year
ago. It is the part of the business where IREN is a landlord to someone else's compute rather
than an operator of its own — the arrangement [[ai-data-center-buildout]] describes as the
structural question hanging over the neoclouds.

## This card follows the filing, not the aggregator

stockanalysis reports a **$192M** operating loss for this quarter. The 10-Q reports **$186M**.

The filing's $368M of total operating expenses nets an $8M gain on disposal of property, plant
and equipment and $2M of other operating income against the charges; the vendor does not. The
chart and every figure on this page are the filed ones, per the rule in
[[sankey-income-statement]] that a filing beats an aggregator whenever they disagree.

The same check caught larger errors on the two other cards built the same day —
[[cloudflare-q3-fy26]] and [[mda-space-q3-fy26]].

## The 80% gross margin is not what it looks like

Cost of revenue is **exclusive of depreciation and amortisation**, which the filing says on its
face. D&A was $148M in the quarter — more than three times the $46M of cost of revenue, and the
largest single cost line in the business.

Carry D&A above the line instead and gross profit is $34M, a **15% margin**, not 80%. This is
the same caveat [[iren-fq4-fy26]] and [[hut-8-q3-fy26]] carry, and it is the reason the margin
on this card cannot be compared with the other cards on the Financials tab.

## What replaced the impairment

The June quarter was dominated by a $450.4M impairment, 62% of that quarter's operating
expenses. This quarter impairment is **$2M**. The loss did not go away with it.

What grew instead is ordinary operating cost. SG&A is **$217M, 95% of revenue**, up from $138M.
D&A is up 74% as the GPUs and buildings come into service. The operating loss widened from $76M
to $186M on a company whose revenue fell.

## The year-ago comparison is a trap

Net income was **+$385M** in the September 2025 quarter. That was not an operating result: a
**$665M unrealised gain on financial instruments** sat below the line, and a $191M tax charge
sat under that. This quarter the same line is a $28M loss.

Operating loss is the honest comparison, and it went from $76M to $186M. This is the same
caution the Financials tab applies to [[alphabet-q3-fy26]], [[coinbase-q3-fy26]] and
[[globalwafers-q2-fy26]]: when one revaluation is larger than the operating result, net
describes the mark.

## A filed quarter, not a derived one

[[iren-fq4-fy26]] had to be derived — IREN's fiscal year ends June 30, so the June quarter is
never filed on its own and that card was the 10-K annual minus the last 10-Q nine-month column.
It said on its face that it would be superseded "in about a week by one that is filed rather
than derived". This is that quarter: the 10-Q was lodged on November 5, 2026, the same day as
the results.

## What this card does not carry

- **No post-result earnings surprise.** The vendor's surprise series still ends at Jun '26, so
  the Sep '26 point on the earnings chart is the pre-announcement consensus (−$0.49), not the
  actual. The filed diluted figure is −$0.66. See [[earnings-surprise-chart]] for why the two
  are on different bases anyway.
- **No trailing or forward P/E** — the last twelve months are a loss and there is no positive
  forward estimate.
- **An ownership donut a quarter behind.** It is built on the Form 13F set for the quarter ended
  Jun 30, 2026, the newest the SEC has published. See [[share-ownership-donut]].
- **Market cap $7.10B** as of November 5, 2026, measured against the share price, so it moves
  every session.

## Context on the day

IREN reported into a session in which the neocloud group sold off hard: the stock fell about 5%
on November 4 after sweeping Democratic midterm wins, and Bloomberg reported on November 5 that
Microsoft is tightening oversight of IREN and Nebius projects. The results landed after that
close. See [[ai-data-center-buildout]] for where IREN's sites sit in the build-out.
