# MDA Space Q3 FY26

**Summary**: MDA Space's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): C$498.1M of revenue, up 22% year over year, C$35.1M of operating profit and C$30.9M of net profit. Guidance went up with the result; backlog went down. The filed gross margin is 25.3%, not the 28.8% the data vendor reports, and the difference is where depreciation sits.

**Sources**: MDA Space interim financial statements and MD&A for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, furnished on Form 6-K November 5, 2026 (source: sec.gov, accession 0001104659-26-122961, exhibits 99.1, 99.2 and 99.5) — consolidated statements of income and the business areas note. Balance sheet and cash flow figures from stockanalysis.com quarterly statements, column 2026-09-30. Market figures from stockanalysis.com, November 5, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-05

## This card follows the filing, and the difference is the gross margin

stockanalysis reports gross profit of **C$143.7M**. The filed statements report **C$125.9M**.

MDA puts **C$17.8M of depreciation and amortisation inside cost of revenue** — it is a named
line there, directly under materials, labour and subcontractors. The vendor takes only the
materials line as cost of revenue and moves the depreciation down into operating expenses.

Operating profit is C$35.1M either way, because the charge is counted either way. Gross margin
is not:

| | filed | as the vendor has it |
|---|---|---|
| Gross profit | **C$125.9M** | C$143.7M |
| Gross margin | **25.3%** | 28.8% |

Three and a half points of margin, on the single ratio a hardware business is judged by. This
page uses the filed figure throughout. The same check caught errors on the two other cards built
the same day, [[iren-fq1-fy27]] and [[cloudflare-q3-fy26]].

Satellite systems is doing essentially all the growing, and is now 72% of the company. Robotics
and space operations — the Canadarm business, the part most people know MDA for — grew 2% and
is being left behind by the rest.

## GAAP against adjusted

The release leads with **adjusted net income of C$60M, up 26% year over year**. That is where
the widely reported C$0.37 of earnings per share comes from, against a C$0.29 consensus. The
filed diluted figure is **C$0.19**.

Adjusted EBITDA was **C$96M**, up 16%, at a 19.3% margin.

Nothing here is wrong, but the two numbers are a factor of about two apart on the same quarter,
and only one of them is in the statements. See [[earnings-surprise-chart]].

## Guidance up, backlog down

Both moved on the same day, in opposite directions.

**Up** — 2026 guidance was raised twice over in the release:
- revenue growth to **18–20%**
- adjusted EBITDA to **C$355–375M**, from C$330–370M, about 13% growth at the midpoint

**Down** — **backlog of C$3.9B** at quarter end, against **C$4.4B** a year earlier. The release
opens on backlog as its measure of revenue visibility, which makes a half-billion decline worth
saying out loud next to the raise.

Two acquisitions are pending: **Blue Canyon Technologies** and **CLS**.

## Balance sheet

C$959.4M of debt against C$2,153.8M of cash at September 30, 2026 — a **net cash position of
C$1,194.4M**, after cash rose from C$397.8M at the June quarter end. The release puts net cash
at C$1.3B and total liquidity at C$2.9B on its own definitions.

Free cash flow for the quarter was **C$65.0M**: C$106.2M of operating cash flow against C$41.2M
of capital expenditure. The release's own non-GAAP free cash flow figure is C$62M, a slightly
different measure.
