# GlobalWafers Q2 FY26

**Summary**: GlobalWafers' second quarter 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026): NT$15,214.3M of revenue, down 5% year over year, a 20.6% gross margin and a 9.4% operating margin — and a 24.8% **net** margin that is almost entirely a mark on its Siltronic stake, not an operating result. This is the newest quarter with a full filed statement; the company presented Q3 figures on November 3, 2026, a day before this card was built.

**Sources**: stockanalysis.com quarterly income statement, balance sheet and cash flow for TPEx 6488, column 2026-06-30 (source: stockanalysis.com/quote/tpex/6488). Q3 2026 figures are from the company's November 3, 2026 investor presentation as reported by Investing.com (source: investing.com, GlobalWafers Q3 2026 slides and earnings call transcript). Exchange rate from Yahoo Finance TWD=X and open.er-api.com, both November 4, 2026.

## Net profit went up 125% while operating profit fell 42%

Everything that matters about this quarter is in those two numbers pointing opposite ways.

| | Q2 FY26 |
|---|---|
| Gross margin | 20.6% |
| Operating margin | **9.4%** |
| Net margin | **24.8%** |

Non-operating income was **NT$3,162.1M — more than twice operating profit** — and is mostly a
mark-to-market gain on the company's holding in Siltronic, the German wafer maker it tried and
failed to acquire in 2022. A year earlier the same line was a small *charge*.

This is the same caution the Financials tab applies to [[alphabet-q3-fy26]] and
[[coinbase-q3-fy26]]: when a single revaluation is larger than the operating result, net margin
describes the mark, and the operating line describes the business. Here the business got
worse — gross profit fell 24% on revenue down 5%, because cost of revenue *rose* 1.6%.

## What the quarter after this one looks like

GlobalWafers presented Q3 2026 on **November 3, 2026**, one day before this card was built.
Taiwan's deadline for the full Q3 consolidated report is November 14, so only deck figures
exist so far and the card is deliberately built on Q2:

| | Q3 FY26 | Q2 FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ~NT$14.9B (+2.6% Y/Y, −2.3% Q/Q) | NT$15.2B |
| Gross margin | 20.0% | 20.6% |
| Operating margin | 9.8% | 9.4% |
| Net margin | **10.1%** | 24.8% |
| EPS | NT$3.13 | NT$7.90 |

Net margin fell from 24.8% to 10.1% **because the Siltronic mark did not repeat**, not because
the business deteriorated — operating margin actually improved. Nine-month revenue is NT$44.1B,
down 4.4% year over year, at a 16.3% net margin against 11.1% a year earlier.

**The sequential decline is a fire.** A mid-July 2026 fire at the Novara plant in Italy held Q3
back; management said the quarter would otherwise have grown by double digits sequentially.
Novara is ramping again.

## Capacity, and who is paying for it

- **All existing wafer capacity, every diameter, is at full utilization.** New capacity in the
  US, Italy and Japan is expected from Q1 2027, with Novara recovering gradually.
- **Record output at the US sites**: four consecutive record months at Sherman, Texas; a record
  Q3 for 12-inch SOI at the Missouri site, which is the company's only US location with
  12-inch SOI capability and is ramping through debottlenecking. Demand for 12-inch SOI already
  exceeds the current expansion plan.
- **A US$1.432 billion GDR**, about four times oversubscribed, was placed alongside the Q3
  presentation. Proceeds are for foreign-currency raw material procurement and global growth,
  and the company says it ended Q3 in a **net cash position of NT$26.3B**.
- **A proposed convertible bond of up to US$500M would be subscribed solely by Micron**,
  alongside a planned ten-year supply agreement. That is the customer financing the supplier's
  capacity, and it is the same cycle [[micron-fq4-fy26]] describes from the other end.

## The adjusted gross margin is a different number

GlobalWafers also reports an adjusted gross margin that **excludes depreciation on major
expansion projects** — about 34% in Q3 against the 20.0% GAAP figure, and 31% in Q1. On a
company whose whole story is building new fabs, that adjustment removes the cost of the thing
being built. The card uses the GAAP figure throughout, for the same reason it does on
[[iren-fq4-fy26]] and [[hut-8-q3-fy26]], where cost of revenue excludes D&A.

## Balance sheet

NT$72.2B of debt ($2,192.7M) against NT$23.8B of cash ($722.0M) at June 30, 2026 — net debt of
$1,470.7M. Free cash flow for the quarter was **−$1.9M**: operating cash flow of $64.9M against
$66.9M of capital expenditure. Capex is consuming essentially all of operating cash flow, which
is what the GDR and the proposed Micron convertible are for.

The debt bars do **not** include the GDR, which closed after the quarter end.

- **No SEC filing behind any of it**, and no US ADR quoted alongside.
- **P/E 28.6 trailing and 28.3 forward**, dividend NT$7.70 a year for a 1.31% yield, both as of
  November 4, 2026 and both measured against the share price, so they move every session.

## Why it is on the watchlist

GlobalWafers is one of the handful of companies that make the silicon the entire AI build-out
is printed on — the substrate under TSMC's wafers, Micron's memory and everything in
[[ai-data-center-buildout]]. The tile is labelled **Semiconductor** rather than letting AI Infra
win by rank, for the same reason [[micron-fq4-fy26]] reads AI Memory: it is what the company
makes, not what the output is used for.
