# Palantir Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Palantir's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $2,313M of revenue, up 96% year over year, an 83% gross margin, $1,085M of operating income and $1,134M of net income. U.S. commercial revenue passed U.S. government revenue for the first time in the company's history. Net margin is higher than operating margin, which is interest income and a 1.2% tax rate, not operations.

**Sources**: Palantir 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 results, filed November 2, 2026 (source: a2026q3ex991pressrelease.htm). Market figures from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-11-02.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

## U.S. commercial passed U.S. government

| Segment | Q3 FY26 | share | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. commercial | 984 | 43% | **+148%** |
| U.S. government | 953 | 41% | +96% |
| International | 376 | 16% | not disclosed |

$984M against $953M — the first time in the company's history, and the headline the release leads
with. U.S. revenue overall was $1,937M, up 119%.

**The International line is derived.** The release publishes only the two U.S. figures and the U.S.
total; International is total revenue minus U.S. revenue, which foots to $2,313M exactly but carries
no year-over-year figure because the release gives none. The four-way split Palantir normally files
— U.S. and international, commercial and government — is in the 10-Q, which was not filed with the
8-K.

## Net margin above operating margin is not an operating result

| Margin | Q3 FY26 |
|---|---|
| Gross | 83.4% |
| Operating | 46.9% |
| **Net** | **49.0%** |

Net margin exceeds operating margin for two reasons, neither of them the business:

- **$63M of net non-operating income** — $89M of interest income less $26M of other expense
- **a 1.2% effective tax rate** — $13.6M of tax on $1,148M of pretax income

Operating income of $1,085M is the figure that describes the business. The same caution the
Financials tab applies to [[alphabet-q3-fy26]] applies here.

## GAAP against adjusted, and where the gap goes

Palantir reports **adjusted income from operations of $1,426.8M (62% margin)** against GAAP
$1,084.8M (47%). The $342.0M difference reconciles exactly:

| | $M |
|---|---|
| Stock-based compensation | 318.9 |
| Employer payroll taxes on it | 23.1 |
| **Total** | **342.0** |

So the 15-point gap between the adjusted and GAAP operating margin **is** stock compensation. SBC was
13.8% of revenue, against $172.3M a year earlier. The release spreads it across cost of revenue
($37.9M), sales and marketing ($123.5M), R&D ($65.2M) and G&A ($92.3M), which is why the chart
attaches it to total operating expenses rather than to one band.

Unusually, **GAAP and adjusted diluted EPS are both $0.44** this quarter, so the street's adjusted
consensus of $0.32 is a like-for-like comparison for once: a beat of $0.12, or 38%. See
[[earnings-surprise-chart]] for why that is normally not safe to assume.

## Balance sheet and guidance

Debt $225.3M against cash and equivalents of $3,619.4M — **net cash $3,394.1M** — plus $1,196.6M of
free cash flow in the quarter. The debt chart's cash bar is cash and equivalents only; Palantir holds
a further $6,942.1M of marketable securities and states **$10.4bn** of cash, equivalents and U.S.
Treasury securities in total, so the chart understates liquidity rather than overstating it.

Guidance: Q4 2026 revenue of $2,649–2,653M and adjusted income from operations of $1,640–1,644M.
Full-year 2026 revenue raised to $8,530–8,534M (+91%) and U.S. commercial revenue to more than
$3,559M, a growth rate of at least 143%.
