# ServiceNow Q3 FY26

**Summary**: ServiceNow's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $4,106M of revenue, up 20.5% year over year, but operating income **down 33%** to $385M and net income down 45% to $277M. The cause is one line — cost of subscription revenue rose 58% against 21% subscription revenue growth.

**Sources**: ServiceNow 8-K exhibit 99.1, filed October 28, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000092/erq3fy26.htm). Market figures from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-10-28.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

## The gross margin is the whole story

Revenue grew 20.5% and gross profit grew 10%. The gap is cost of subscription revenue, which went
from $666M to $1,054M — **up 58% on 21% subscription growth**. Subscription gross margin fell from
79.8% to 73.6%.

That is the shape of a software company buying compute. ServiceNow raised its AI annual contract
value target for 2026 to $1.7 billion, up 70% from the start of the year; the cost of serving it
arrives on the cost of revenue line before the contract value does.

Operating expenses grew more slowly than revenue — 21.8% against 20.5% — so the operating margin
collapse is not a spending problem. Had subscription cost of revenue grown in line with
subscription revenue, at $806M rather than $1,054M, operating income would have been about $633M
and the margin 15%.

## Professional services runs at a loss

$157M of cost against $118M of revenue, a gross loss of $39M. That is normal for the line and it
is 3% of the company, but it means the Financials card's two revenue bands are not two
profit centres — one of them subtracts.

## Below the line

Interest expense went from $6M to $81M year over year, and interest income fell from $115M to
$69M. Net non-operating is −$15M against +$122M a year ago, which takes another $137M out of the
year-over-year comparison before tax.

## Guidance

ServiceNow raised full-year subscription revenue guidance to $15,780M-$15,790M, about 21% constant
currency growth, and guided Q4 subscription revenues of $4,245M-$4,255M. Current remaining
performance obligations were $13.65 billion, up 20.5%.
