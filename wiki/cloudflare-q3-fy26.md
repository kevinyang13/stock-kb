# Cloudflare Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Cloudflare's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $782.5M of revenue, up 39% year over year, a 71% gross margin, a $67.9M operating loss and a $34.1M net loss. The quarter is the first Cloudflare card to carry a revenue split, and the first to show the $11.0M restructuring charge that the data vendor leaves out of operating expenses entirely.

**Sources**: Cloudflare 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed November 5, 2026 (source: sec.gov, accession 0001477333-26-000061) — condensed consolidated statements of operations and disaggregation of revenue note (R37). Balance sheet and cash flow figures from stockanalysis.com quarterly statements, column 2026-09-30. Reported EPS and consensus from TheFly, November 5, 2026. Market figures from stockanalysis.com, November 5, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-05

## This card follows the filing, not the aggregator

stockanalysis reports a **$56.9M** operating loss for this quarter. The 10-Q reports **$67.9M**.

The difference is exactly **$11,018K** — the restructuring and other charges line, which the
vendor leaves out of total operating expenses altogether. Gross profit and net loss agree; only
the operating line does not, because the charge reappears below it.

This is the same failure mode as the Meta case recorded in [[sankey-income-statement]], where
the vendor put a $9,890M charge below the operating line and reported $22,878M of operating
income against the 8-K's $12,988M. The chart here draws restructuring as its own band so the
charge is visible rather than netted away.

Both other cards built on November 5, 2026 hit the same problem: [[iren-fq1-fy27]] and
[[mda-space-q3-fy26]].

## The split, at last

The previous Cloudflare card, Q2 FY26, carried no segment split at all, on the grounds that
Cloudflare publishes no product or business segments. That is still true — but it does file a
geographic disaggregation that foots to the dollar, and this card uses it:

| Region | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 | Y/Y | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States | $407.9M | $282.6M | **+44%** | 52% |
| EMEA | $202.2M | $153.3M | +32% | 26% |
| Asia Pacific | $111.7M | $84.7M | +32% | 14% |
| Other | $60.7M | $41.5M | +46% | 8% |
| **Total** | **$782.5M** | **$562.0M** | **+39%** | 100% |

The United States is growing fastest of the three large regions and has gone from 50% to 52% of
revenue in a year. This is the same move [[datadog-q3-fy26]] made — a geographic split that was
always filed and simply had not been fetched.

The note also splits by sales channel: **channel partners $247.4M** (32% of revenue, up from
27%) and **direct customers $535.1M** (68%). The channel is taking share of the mix.

## GAAP against adjusted

Cloudflare's release reported earnings per share of **38c against a 34c consensus** — a beat,
and that is the number the wires carried on the day. The filed GAAP diluted figure for the same
quarter is a **loss of $0.10 a share**.

Both are true; they are different bases. The income statement on this card is GAAP throughout,
which is why its net line is a loss while the headline was a beat. [[earnings-surprise-chart]]
sets out why the two can never be put on the same axis.

The vendor's own consensus for this quarter, $0.02, is on a third basis again, so no surprise
figure is computed for Q3 on this card at all.

## Growth is accelerating, losses with it

| | Q3 FY26 | Q2 FY26 | Q3 FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $782.5M | $696M | $562.0M |
| Revenue Y/Y | **+39%** | +36% | — |
| Gross margin | 71% | 72% | 74% |
| Operating margin | (9%) | — | (7%) |
| Net margin | (4%) | (24%) | (0%) |

Revenue growth went up, from 36% to 39%. Gross margin came down three points in a year, as cost
of revenue grew 54% against revenue's 39% — the cost of running more network. Operating loss
widened faster than revenue grew.

The net margin improved sharply from the June quarter, but that quarter's $170M loss was its own
story; this one's $34.1M loss is closer to the run rate.

## Balance sheet

$4,707.5M of debt against $1,969.5M of cash at September 30, 2026 — net debt of **$2,738.0M**.
Free cash flow for the quarter was **+$93.6M**: $215.4M of operating cash flow against $121.8M
of capital expenditure.

That net debt figure counts cash only. Cloudflare holds a further **$3,159.2M of short-term
investments**, which would more than cover the gap.

## What this card does not carry
