# Apple FQ4 FY26

**Summary**: Apple's fiscal fourth quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 26, 2026): $114,477M of revenue, up 12% year over year, a 47.9% gross margin, $35,631M of operating income and $30,711M of net income. A record September quarter — but operating margin slipped slightly despite a wider gross margin, because research and development grew 32% against revenue's 12%.

**Sources**: Apple 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q4 fiscal 2026 results, filed November 2, 2026 (source: a8-kex991q4202609262026.htm). Market figures from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-11-02.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

## Gross margin widened, operating margin did not

| Margin | FQ4 FY26 | FQ4 FY25 |
|---|---|---|
| Gross | 47.9% | 47.2% |
| Operating | 31.1% | 31.6% |
| Net | 26.8% | 26.8% |

Gross margin improved by 0.7 points, helped by Services, which carries a far higher margin than
hardware and is now 28% of revenue. Operating margin still fell by half a point, because operating
expenses grew 21% against revenue's 12%.

Almost all of that is **R&D, up 32% to $11,730M** — more than $2.8bn of extra spend in a single
quarter, against SG&A's +6%. The release does not say what it is for, so this page does not
speculate; it is simply the line that moved.

The tax rate fell from 16.3% to 15.0%, which is why net income grew 12% while operating income grew
10%.
