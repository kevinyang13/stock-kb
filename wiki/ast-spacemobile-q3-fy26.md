# AST SpaceMobile Q3 FY26

**Summary**: AST SpaceMobile's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $42.3M of revenue, up 187% year over year, against $211.0M of total operating expenses and a $171.8M net loss. Revenue nearly tripled and the loss still widened. This is the first ASTS card to carry a revenue split, which was always filed and simply had not been fetched.

**Sources**: AST SpaceMobile 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed November 9, 2026 (source: sec.gov, accession 0001193125-26-453759) — condensed consolidated statements of operations (R4). Balance sheet and cash flow figures from stockanalysis.com quarterly statements, column 2026-09-30. Market figures from stockanalysis.com, November 9, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

## ASTS reports no gross profit line

The statement of operations has one revenue line and then goes straight to total operating
expenses. There is no cost of revenue subtotal and no gross profit.

The chart derives one. The 10-Q discloses **product cost of revenues of $22,451K and service
cost of revenues of $2,863K** as components inside total operating expenses, and those two
reconcile the statement exactly:

```
cost of revenues          25,314
five named expense lines 185,712
                        --------
total operating expenses 211,026   <- the filed subtotal, to the dollar
```

So gross profit of $17.0M, a 40% margin, is a construction of this card and not a filed figure.
It is stated on the card itself. The same situation arises on the two utility cards built the
same day, [[constellation-energy-q3-fy26]] and [[vistra-q3-fy26]], for the same reason and with
the same caveat.

## The split, at last

| Line | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 | Y/Y | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Service | $21.8M | $7.0M | **+211%** | 51% |
| Product | $20.6M | $7.7M | +166% | 49% |
| **Total** | **$42.3M** | **$14.7M** | **+187%** | 100% |

The previous ASTS card carried "not split by segment". That was never right: the product and
service disaggregation is on the face of the statement. Service passed product this quarter for
the first time.

The margins behind them are very different. Service cost of revenues was $2.9M on $21.8M of
service revenue; product cost of revenues was $22.5M on $20.6M of product revenue — a **product
gross loss**. All of the derived gross profit comes from service.

## Revenue tripled and the loss grew

| | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $42.3M | $14.7M |
| Operating loss | **($168.7M)** | ($79.7M) |
| Net loss before NCI | ($171.8M) | ($163.8M) |

Engineering services costs alone were **$95.8M, more than twice revenue**, and grew 135% while
revenue grew 187%. The operating loss more than doubled.

The net loss barely moved year over year only because the September 2025 quarter carried a
$91.4M charge in "other expense, net" that this quarter does not. Operating loss is again the
honest comparison.

## The noncontrolling interest is large

$39.4M of the $171.8M loss — 23% of it — is allocated to the noncontrolling interest, leaving
$132.4M attributable to common stockholders. The filed per-share figure is computed on the
latter. Both numbers are real; the chart draws the $171.8M because that is the statement's own
bottom line before the allocation.

## Balance sheet

**$4,118.7M of debt against $2,798.9M of cash** at September 30, 2026 — net debt of $1,319.8M.
Free cash flow for the quarter was **−$464.2M**, eleven times the quarter's revenue.

Set beside [[rocket-lab-q3-fy26]], filed the same day: Rocket Lab burned $23.9M against $4.4B of
cash and almost no debt. Two space companies, opposite balance sheets.
