# Alphabet Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Alphabet's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $127,577M of revenue, up 25% year over year, a 60% gross margin and $40,576M of operating income, up 30%. Net income **fell** 24% to $26,468M, entirely because of a swing in other income from +$12,759M a year ago to −$8,221M.

**Sources**: Alphabet 8-K exhibit 99.1, filed October 28, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000080/googexhibit991q32026.htm). Market figures from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-10-28.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

## The distortion runs the other way this time

[[sankey-income-statement]] records Alphabet as the standing example of a one-off gain pushing net
margin above operating margin. This quarter it is the reverse, and it is larger. Operating income
grew 30%; net income fell 24%. The whole of that difference sits on one line: other income and
expense went from **+$12,759M** in Q3 2025 to **−$8,221M** in Q3 2026, a $20,980M swing, none of
which touches the operating business.

Read the operating margin. The card says so in its own words, because a 21% net margin against a
32% operating margin invites a conclusion about the business that the business did not produce.

## Segments

Alphabet reports six revenue lines plus a hedging line.

| Line | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search & other | 63,350 | 56,567 | +12% |
| Google Cloud | 30,709 | 15,157 | +103% |
| Google subscriptions, platforms and devices | 14,290 | 12,870 | +11% |
| YouTube ads | 11,207 | 10,261 | +9% |
| Google Network | 7,455 | 7,354 | +1% |
| Other Bets | 372 | 344 | +8% |
| Hedging gains (losses) | 194 | (207) | — |
| **Total revenues** | **127,577** | **102,346** | **+25%** |

**Cloud more than doubled.** $30,709M against $15,157M, and Alphabet attributes part of it to
sales of TPU systems — hardware revenue, not consumption. Cloud is now 24% of the company, against
15% a year ago, and its operating income went from $3,594M to $11,121M.

Search grew 12%, which Alphabet says is its fastest Search query growth in five years. The six
lines sum to $127,383M; the $194M gap to total revenue is the hedging line, which is recognised at
the consolidated level and not allocated to any segment.

## The quarter's financing

Alphabet issued **$28.7B** of senior unsecured notes in the quarter and raised $12.4B more through
an at-the-market equity program ($4.3B of Class A, $8.1B of Class C). It also paid its first
preferred dividend, $298M, on mandatory convertible preferred stock issued earlier in the year.
Long-term debt went from $46,547M at December 31, 2025 to $125,102M at September 30, 2026.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was $51,355M against $23,953M a year ago, and operating cash
flow was $42,290M — so free cash flow was **negative $9,065M** for the quarter.
