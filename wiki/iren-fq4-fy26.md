# IREN FQ4 FY26

**Summary**: IREN's fourth fiscal quarter 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026): $137.2M of revenue, down 27% year over year, and a $684.0M net loss of which a $450.4M asset impairment is the bulk. The quarter is the first in which AI Cloud revenue exceeded Bitcoin Mining revenue — $70.5M against $66.7M. This is IREN's first card on the Financials tab.

**Sources**: IREN 10-K for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, filed August 27, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000052), and the 10-Q for the nine months ended March 31, 2026, filed May 8, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000026). Debt, cash and free cash flow from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-10-28.

## The quarter is derived, not filed

IREN's fiscal year ends June 30, so **the June quarter is never reported on its own**. A company
with a December year-end files a Q4 8-K; a June-year company's fourth quarter appears only inside
the annual figures. Every number on this page is the twelve months in the 10-K minus the nine
months in the last 10-Q.

That is a derivation, so it has to prove itself. It does, three ways: revenue comes to exactly
$137,225K, pretax to $(691,968)K and net to $(683,999)K, and the two reported segments foot to the
revenue total to the dollar. An independent aggregator reading the same filings reports $137.23M,
$(691.97)M and $(684.00)M. Nothing here is estimated.

This also means the card is **not the most recent quarter that exists**. IREN had not published
its FQ1 FY27 results, for the quarter ended September 30 2026, when this was built; the
confirmed date is **November 5, 2026**. The September quarter is reported nowhere yet, and in
about a week this card is superseded by one that is filed rather than derived.

## The gross margin is not what it looks like

IREN reports **cost of revenue exclusive of depreciation and amortisation**, and carries D&A as a
separate operating line. For most companies that distinction is cosmetic. For IREN it is the whole
picture: D&A was **$112.1M in the quarter against $33.3M of cost of revenue** — more than three
times larger.

A bitcoin miner's economics live in its hardware. Depreciation of that hardware *is* the cost of
production in any ordinary sense, and moving it below the gross profit line produces a 76% gross
margin for a business whose operating loss is four and a half times its revenue. Carrying D&A
above the line instead would put gross profit at a **loss**.

The 76% on the card is faithful to the filing. It is not comparable to the gross margins on the
other cards on the Financials tab, and the card says so.

## The impairment is the quarter

Of $724.3M in total operating expenses, **$450.4M is a single impairment of assets** — 62% of
operating costs and 328% of revenue. IREN took $638.8M of impairments across FY26 and $450.4M of
it landed in this one quarter.

Strip it out and the operating loss is around $170M, still a loss but a different kind of number.
This is the distortion [[alphabet-q3-fy26]] documents, in reverse: a one-off charge rather than a
one-off gain, but the same lesson — a margin built on top of it describes an event, not a business.

## AI Cloud passed Bitcoin Mining

The segment table is the reason to look at this quarter at all.

| Segment | FQ4 FY26 | share | FQ4 FY25 | share | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI Cloud | 70,515 | 51.4% | 6,963 | 3.7% | +913% |
| Bitcoin Mining | 66,710 | 48.6% | 180,329 | 96.3% | −63% |

A year ago AI Cloud was a rounding error at 3.7% of revenue. It is now the larger segment. Over the
full fiscal year AI Cloud was $128.8M against Bitcoin Mining's $578.2M, so the crossover is a
quarter-end event, not a year-long trend — most of that AI Cloud revenue arrived in the last three
months.

**Total revenue still fell 27%**, because mining fell faster than AI Cloud grew. In absolute terms
mining lost $113.6M of quarterly revenue and AI Cloud added $63.6M. A transition, not yet a
replacement.

## Balance sheet

$7,839.5M of debt against $5,895.6M of cash, so net debt $1,943.9M, with a further $1,670.3M of
restricted cash held separately. The debt is $7,423.6M long-term, $169.4M current, plus $246.6M of
leases.

Free cash flow was **−$167.1M**: $1,811.1M of operating cash flow against **$1,978.2M of capital
expenditure**. Against $137.2M of quarterly revenue, IREN spent roughly **fourteen times its
quarterly revenue on capex in the same three months**. The balance sheet, not the income
statement, is where this company currently is.
