# Intel Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Intel's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 26, 2026): $16,513M of revenue, up 21% year over year, with operating income up 270% to $2,522M. Data Center & AI grew 78% and is now 44% of revenue. Net income of $3,520M is larger than operating income, because $1,460M of it sits below the operating line.

**Sources**: Intel 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 results, filed October 29, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000188).

**Last updated**: 2026-10-29

## The operating recovery is real; the net line is not the way to see it

Operating income nearly quadrupled, on gross margin that went from 38% to 43% while revenue grew
21% and cost of sales grew 12%. That is an operating story.

**Net income still fell 18%**, because the year-ago quarter had $3,670M of interest and other
income against $1,130M this time. And net margin reads 21% against a 15% operating margin, because
$1,460M of this quarter's profit — the equity gains plus interest and other — sits below the
operating line.

|---|---|---|

| All Other | $0.7B | −30% |
| Intersegment eliminations | $(6.3)B | |
| **Total net revenue** | **$16.5B** | **+21%** |

DCAI grew 78% and is closing on CCPG, which fell 5%. **Intel reports business-unit revenue
inclusive of intersegment sales**, and Foundry's $6.7B is almost entirely internal — the gross
figures sum to $22.8B against $16.5B of consolidated revenue, so the Financials card draws CCPG and
DCAI with Foundry-external-and-other as the residual.

## The share count

Diluted shares went from 4,531M to 5,307M, up 17%, after a **$23 billion public equity offering**.
Net income attributable to Intel fell 18% and EPS fell 33%, the gap being dilution.
