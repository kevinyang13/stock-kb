# Datadog Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Datadog's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $1,197.1M of revenue, up 35% year over year, an 80.2% gross margin, $74.7M of operating income and $118.3M of net income. The operating line was a $5.8M loss a year ago. Net margin is higher than operating margin, which is interest income and a 5% tax rate, not the business. This is the first Datadog card to draw a revenue split.

**Sources**: Datadog 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 results, filed November 4, 2026 (source: ex-991x20260930x8k.htm). The revenue split is from Datadog's 10-Q for the same quarter, filed the same day (source: ddog-20260930.htm, revenue by geographic area). Prior-period figures are the September 30, 2025 column of the release.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-04

## The split is by geography, and it was always filed

The previous card for this ticker drew a single "Total revenue" bar and the note said Datadog
discloses revenue by geography only. Both halves were true — and the split still never made it
onto the chart. It does now:

| | Q3 FY26 | share | Q3 FY25 | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North America | 881.4 | 74% | 633.9 | **+39%** |
| International | 315.7 | 26% | 251.7 | +25% |
| **Total** | **1,197.1** | **100%** | **885.7** | **+35%** |

The United States alone is $844.6M of the North America figure. Datadog reports **one operating
segment**, so this is a geographic disaggregation rather than a segment table — the chart says so
and [[sankey-income-statement]] treats that as the honest fallback when a filer publishes no
segments. North America is growing faster than International, so the mix is concentrating rather
than diversifying.

## Net margin above operating margin is not an operating result

| Margin | Q3 FY26 |
|---|---|
| Gross | 80.2% |
| Operating | 6.2% |
| **Net** | **9.9%** |

Net margin exceeds operating margin for two reasons, neither of them the business:

- **$49.9M of net non-operating income** — $53.1M of interest and other income less $3.2M of
  interest expense
- **a 5.0% effective tax rate** — $6.3M of tax on $124.6M of pretax income

Operating income of $74.7M is the figure that describes the business. The same caution the
Financials tab applies to [[palantir-q3-fy26]] and [[alphabet-q3-fy26]] applies here. What is
genuinely new is the *sign*: the operating line was a **$5.8M loss** in the same quarter a year
ago, which is why the chart's year-ago hover omits it — a percentage change against a negative
base is not meaningful.

## Where the gross profit goes

R&D is **$491.6M, 41% of revenue** — larger than cost of revenue, sales and marketing or G&A, and
more than twice the cost of delivering the service. Datadog spends 51% of its gross profit on
research and development.

Stock compensation is **$237.2M, 20% of revenue**, and the release splits it by line:

| | $M |
|---|---|
| Cost of revenue | 9.5 |
| Research and development | 146.4 |
| Sales and marketing | 49.2 |
| General and administrative | 32.1 |
| **Total** | **237.2** |

Because it spans four lines, the chart attaches it to total operating expenses rather than to any
one band. R&D alone carries $146.4M of it — 30% of the R&D line is stock.

On the chart, **SG&A is general and administrative alone ($90M)**; sales and marketing is drawn
as its own $303M band. The same convention was used for [[super-micro-fq1-fy27]].

## Free cash flow has two definitions on this card

The debt chart's **$279.1M** of free cash flow is operating cash flow less capital expenditure —
$290.0M less $10.9M. Datadog's own release states **$251M**, which also deducts capitalised
software development costs. Both start from the same $290M of operating cash flow; the chart uses
the vendor's definition so that it is comparable with the other 71 cards.

## Balance sheet

Debt $1,307.2M (a $986.6M convertible note plus $320.7M of leases) against $881.2M of cash and
equivalents — **net debt $426.0M**. The debt chart's cash bar is cash and equivalents only;
Datadog holds a further **$4,352.4M of short-term investments**, which appear on the bar's hover
but are not added into it, so the chart understates liquidity rather than overstating it.

## What this card does not carry

- **The earnings chart's Sep '26 column has no actual.** Datadog reported hours before this page
  was written and the vendor has not posted its figure. The consensus shown is $0.18 from 10
  analysts, against GAAP diluted EPS of $0.32 — a gap that is basis, not surprise. See
  [[earnings-surprise-chart]].
- **P/E** is measured against the share price as of November 4, 2026 and moves every session:
  263.4 trailing, 68.2 forward. Datadog pays no dividend.

## Guidance

Q4 2026 revenue of $1.19B–$1.20B, and full-year 2026 revenue of $4.515B–$4.525B.
