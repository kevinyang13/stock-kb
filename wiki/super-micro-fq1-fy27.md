# Super Micro FQ1 FY27

**Summary**: Super Micro's first quarter of fiscal 2027 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $19,946M of net sales, up 298% year over year, a 12.7% gross margin, $2,064M of operating income and $1,594M of net income. Revenue nearly quadrupled in a year and grew 79% in a single quarter, while gross margin fell from 17.5% to 12.7%. The figures are explicitly **preliminary and unaudited**.

**Sources**: Super Micro 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q1 fiscal 2027 results, filed November 3, 2026 (source: exhibit991_20260930.htm). Prior-period figures are the September 30, 2025 column of the same release. Balance sheet and cash flow figures for the debt chart from stockanalysis.com, column 2026-09-30.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-03

---

## These figures are preliminary

Super Micro's release states that its financial closing procedures for the quarter are **not
complete**, that the data "are not a comprehensive statement of the Company's results", that its
independent registered public accounting firm "has not audited, reviewed, compiled or performed any
procedures with respect to such preliminary data", and that final results "may differ materially"
(source: exhibit991_20260930.htm).

No other card on the Financials tab carries that warning. Every figure below should be read as
subject to revision when the 10-Q is filed. The release also notes that certain prior period amounts
have been reclassified to conform to the current presentation.

## Volume, not margin

| | FQ1 FY27 | FQ4 FY26 | FQ1 FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net sales | $19.9B | $11.1B | $5.0B |
| Gross margin | **12.7%** | 17.5% | 9.3% |
| Net income | $1,594M | $1,178M | $168M |
| Diluted EPS | $1.75 | $1.62 | $0.26 |

Gross margin is the lowest of any card on the Financials tab. It is up from 9.3% a year ago but
**down 480 basis points from the quarter before**, on revenue that grew 79% sequentially. The
company is converting a much larger book of business at a materially thinner rate than it did three
months ago.

The release does not explain the sequential decline. It should not be inferred from the revenue
growth alone.

What saves the quarter is the expense base. Operating expenses are **$477M, or 2.4% of revenue**, so
81% of a 12.7% gross margin survives to a 10.3% operating margin. A year ago operating expenses were
5.7% of revenue. Super Micro is a systems assembler, not a chip designer, and the chart's shape
shows it: a very wide cost-of-revenue band and three narrow expense threads.

Stock-based compensation is $100.2M of that $477M — more than a fifth of all operating expense, and
up only 12% year over year while revenue grew 298%.

## No segment split

The release discloses no revenue breakdown of any kind, and the 10-Q for this quarter is not filed.
The FY26 10-K disclosed net sales by geography only, which is why the previous card also had none.
The chart therefore draws a single "Total revenue" bar, which is a fallback rather than a default —
see [[sankey-income-statement]].

## Balance sheet and guidance

Total bank debt and convertible notes were **$8.6B** against **$6.9B** of cash at September 30, 2026;
the debt chart's $8,646.0M and $6,897.5M match the release's own rounding. Free cash flow for the
quarter was **−$475.6M**: the release reports $434M used in operations plus $47M of capital
expenditures and investments.

Guidance is $19.5B–$21.5B of net sales for FQ2 FY27, GAAP diluted EPS of $1.59–$1.83, and
**$72.0B–$82.0B of net sales for fiscal 2027**. The $77.0B midpoint of that range leaves $57.1B for the
three quarters still to come, or $19.0B each — so the guidance implies this quarter's step change
holds rather than continues.
