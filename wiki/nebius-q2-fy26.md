# Nebius Group Q2 FY26

**Summary**: Nebius Group's Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026): $582.3M revenue, up 454% year over year, at a 77% gross margin — and a $190.4M net loss. Revenue is almost entirely one segment, the Nebius AI cloud, and the loss is almost entirely depreciation and interest on the data centres that produce it.

**Sources**: Nebius Group 6-K exhibit 99.1, filed August 12, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926094844/nbis-20260812xex99d1.htm). Price, 52-week range and quote data from stockanalysis.com and the IBKR connector, both taken 2026-10-26. Headlines from stockanalysis.com's NBIS page, 2026-10-26.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-26

## Where the loss comes from

The gross margin is 77%, so the business is not losing money on what it sells. Two lines below
gross profit account for the whole result:

- **Depreciation and amortization, $259.7M** — 45% of revenue, and larger than the net loss itself.
  This is the cost of the GPU fleet and the data centres passing through the P&L.
- **Interest expense, $119.1M** — against $24.1M of interest income, a net $95.0M financing charge.

Strip both out and the quarter is comfortably profitable: adjusted EBITDA was $236.2M, against a
$21.0M loss a year earlier. That gap between $236.2M of adjusted EBITDA and a $190.4M net loss
*is* the business model, not a presentational quirk — the capital has to be bought before it can be
rented out.

The scale of that buying is the number to hold onto: **purchases of property, equipment and
intangibles were $8,130.3M in the first half of 2026**, against $981.3M of first-half revenue.
Capex ran at roughly eight times revenue. That is what the market is pricing, and what the debt
headlines below are about.

Adjusted EBITDA by segment shows where it is earned and spent: AI cloud +$285.7M, Avride −$40.1M,
TripleTen −$9.4M.

## Debt level and coverage

The second chart on the Financials card, for the same quarter. Debt and cash are the June 30, 2026
balance sheet; free cash flow is that quarter's flow.

| | Q2 FY26 |
|---|---|
| Debt | $10,195M |
| Cash and equivalents | $8,042M |
| Free cash flow | $(3,411)M |
| **Net debt** | **$2,153M** |

Cash covers 79% of debt, so the balance sheet itself is not stretched. What the chart shows is that
the coverage is **shrinking from the inside**: the quarter burned $3.4B of free cash flow, which is
42% of the cash pile in three months. Debt went from $49.5M at the end of FY2024 to $4,973M at the
end of FY2025 to $10,195M here — roughly doubling again in half a year — and the $3.5B Citi
facility announced in October extends that line further.

The quarter's capex of $5,657M reconciles with the 6-K's first-half figure of $8,130.3M, so the
Q1 figure was about $2,473M. The burn is accelerating, not steady.

## Sources disagree: operating income

stockanalysis.com reports Q2 FY26 operating income of **−$1.3M**. The filing reports a loss from
operations of **−$175.9M**. The difference is roughly $174.6M that the aggregator moves out of
product development and SG&A — product development reads 75.1 against the filing's 191.0, and SG&A
115.2 against 173.9. **What that $174.6M is has not been established.** Share-based compensation
was $102.5M that quarter (source: stockanalysis.com cash flow statement), so it accounts for at
most 59% of the gap and is not the explanation on its own.
The same site's "EBT excluding unusual items" of −$63.5M against a pretax loss of −$176.7M is the
other half of the same adjustment.

**The filing is used** on the chart and on this page. Both sources agree on revenue ($582.3M), gross
profit ($448.7M), net loss ($190.4M) and EPS (−$0.68).

## What is moving the stock

As of the October 26, 2026 close, NBIS is **$153.27, down 3.45% on the day** and 49% below its
52-week high of $299.86. Two live items:

- **October 26** — Elon Musk said more than 200 AI neoclouds face "guppy-level death"; CRWV, NBIS
  and IREN all fell together (source: CNBC, Reuters, via stockanalysis.com).
- **October 22** — Nebius secured a **$3.5B financing facility with Citi** for data centre growth.
  The stock fell on it: at eight times revenue in capex, more debt capacity reads as more risk
  rather than more runway (source: Reuters, Business Wire).

Next earnings is **not confirmed**. Sources split between November 10 and November 11, 2026;
`events.json` carries November 10 flagged `(est.)`.
