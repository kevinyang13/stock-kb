# Ouster Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Ouster's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $59.3M of revenue, up 50% year over year, a 43% gross margin and a $14.2M net loss, narrowed from $21.7M a year ago. A fifteenth consecutive quarter of product revenue growth, and the first where the statement's royalty line is material. Two thirds of the year-over-year improvement in the loss is operating; the rest is interest income on a cash balance that doubled.

**Sources**: Ouster 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 results, filed November 3, 2026 (source: exhibit991-9302026.htm). Prior-period figures are the three months ended September 30, 2025 column of the same release. Balance sheet and cash flow figures for the debt chart from stockanalysis.com, column 2026-09-30.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-03

**The press release's five columns are not in the order they look.** The header reads *Three Months
Ended September 30 (2026, 2025) · Three Months Ended June 30 (2026) · Nine Months Ended September 30
(2026, 2025)*, so the second column is the year-ago quarter and the third is last quarter. Reading
column three as the comparative puts last quarter's R&D, G&A and net loss into the year-ago slot —
which inverts the R&D trend from +4% to −4%.

## The revenue split is by type, not by segment

Ouster reports a single operating segment, so there is no segment table. The statement itself
disaggregates revenue into two lines, and the chart draws them:

| Line | Q3 FY26 | share | Q3 FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product revenue | 56.0 | 94% | 39.5 |
| Royalties | 3.3 | 6% | 0.04 |

Royalties were $38 **thousand** a year ago. The percentage change is not worth printing; the fact
that the line exists at all is the change. The previous card for this ticker said "no segment
split", which was true of segments and missed this.

More than 27,000 lidar and camera sensors shipped for revenue, of which lidar was approximately 27%
of units — so the majority of units are now Stereolabs cameras, acquired during the year. Goodwill
of $38.5M appears on the balance sheet where there was none at December 31, 2025.

## Gross margin: 43% against 49%, but not really

GAAP gross margin was 43%, against 42% a year ago and 49% in the quarter before. Ouster states that
**a one-time refund added approximately 1,000 basis points to that 49%** (source:
exhibit991-9302026.htm). Stripping it out leaves roughly 39% last quarter against 43% now, which
points the other way from the printed pair.

Non-GAAP gross margin — excluding stock compensation and amortization of acquired intangibles — was
47%, flat against a year ago and down from 53%.

## Where the improvement came from

The net loss narrowed by $7.577M year over year. Two thirds of that is the operating loss shrinking
and one third is not operating at all:

| Source of the improvement | $M |
|---|---|
| Operating loss, $(24.251)M to $(19.260)M | **+4.991** |
| Total other income, $2.590M to $4.385M | **+1.795** |
| Tax, a $72K charge to a $719K benefit | **+0.791** |
| **Total** | **+7.577** |

The $1.795M of non-operating improvement is almost all interest income, up 72% to $4.159M because
the cash and investments balance roughly doubled: $421M of cash, restricted cash and short-term
investments at September 30, 2026, against $210M at December 31, 2025. That is a financing outcome,
not an operating one.

For scale, interest income alone now covers 22% of the operating loss.

Income tax was a **$0.7M benefit** rather than a charge, which is why the chart's non-operating
income bar reads $5.0M where the statement's total other income is $4.4M — the loss layout pools
non-operating income and the tax benefit into one inflow, because both fund the same charges.

## The debt chart's cash bar is narrower than Ouster's liquidity

The bar is cash and cash equivalents only, $88.9M. The $330.9M of short-term investments and $0.3M
of restricted cash are named on the bar's hover but not added into it. Together they make the $421M
Ouster's release quotes.

The $15.4M of "debt" is entirely lease liabilities — $4.9M current, $10.4M long-term. Ouster has no
borrowings. Free cash flow for the quarter was −$35.0M.

## What this card does not carry

- **The earnings chart's Sep '26 column has no actual.** Ouster reported hours before this page was
  written and the vendor has not posted its figure. The consensus shown is $(0.30), from a single
  analyst, against GAAP diluted EPS of $(0.20).
- **No P/E, trailing or forward.** The last twelve months are a loss and there is no positive
  forward estimate, so both read `n/a`, which is correct rather than missing.
- **Guidance**: $61M–$64M of total revenue for the fourth quarter of 2026.
