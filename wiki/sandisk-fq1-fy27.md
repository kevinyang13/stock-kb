# Sandisk FQ1 FY27

**Summary**: Sandisk's fiscal first quarter 2027 (quarter ended October 2, 2026): $11,031M of revenue, up 378% year over year, an 85% gross margin and $8,101M of net income. Datacenter revenue went from $269M to $4,802M in a year. The company also announced an eight-for-one forward stock split.

**Sources**: Sandisk 8-K exhibit 99.1, fiscal Q1 2027 results, filed October 29, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2023554/000162828026068243). Share prices and peer moves from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-11-02. Kioxia figures from its fiscal Q2 2026 results of October 30, 2026 as reported by Investing.com and Seoul Economic Daily. Flash Ventures ownership from Sandisk's FY2026 Form 10-K (source: sndk-20260703.htm). Management quotes from the fiscal Q1 2027 earnings call of October 29, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

## The whole quarter is price

Revenue grew 378%. **Cost of revenue grew 2%** — from $1,621M to $1,653M. Sandisk sold roughly the
same quantity of NAND at vastly higher prices, and almost every incremental dollar fell to gross
profit.

That is what an 85% gross margin on a commodity memory business means. It is a pricing artifact,
not a structural one, and it unwinds when pricing does. The same caution applies to
[[micron-fq4-fy26]], whose 87% gross margin comes from the same cycle.

## Why the stock fell while the quarter was a record

The shares went from **$1,404.77 on Oct 28 to $1,075.08 on Nov 2, down 23% in three sessions**
(source: stockanalysis.com, read 2026-11-02).

| Session | Close | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 28, 2026 | 1,404.77 | +7.66% |
| Oct 29, 2026 — results | 1,330.05 | −5.32% |
| Oct 30, 2026 — Kioxia reports | 1,150.89 | **−13.47%** |
| Nov 2, 2026 | 1,075.08 | −6.59% |

The single worst day was not the results. It was **Oct 30, when Kioxia reported** — and Kioxia is
not a peer, it is the manufacturer. The two co-own **Flash Ventures**, the three Japanese
joint-venture entities (Flash Partners, Flash Alliance, Flash Forward) that supply *all* of
Sandisk's flash wafers, split 49.9% Sandisk and 50.1% Kioxia, extended in January 2026 through
December 31, 2034 (source: sndk-20260703.htm, Sandisk FY2026 Form 10-K; Kioxia and Sandisk joint
announcement, January 29, 2026).

Kioxia's own quarter was also a record — revenue ¥2,480.0bn, operating profit ¥1,994.7bn — but:

- its **average selling price rose in the mid-20% range** in July–September, against roughly **70%**
  the quarter before
- it guided October–December adjusted operating profit to about **¥2.0tn against a consensus near
  ¥2.35tn**, roughly 15% below, which implies **NAND prices approximately flat quarter on quarter**
- its shares fell **15.8% in Tokyo on Nov 2**

(source: Kioxia fiscal Q2 2026 results of October 30, 2026, as reported by Investing.com and Seoul
Economic Daily.)

So the market did not revise this quarter. It repriced the **pricing assumption** behind the 85%
gross margin the section above describes — the one that "unwinds when pricing does".

### It is NAND-specific, not memory-wide

| | Oct 28 → Nov 2 | Oct 30 | Nov 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sandisk | −23% | −13.5% | −6.6% |
| [[micron-fq4-fy26]] | −15% | −5.3% | −5.8% |
| SK Hynix | — | −7.1% | **+3.8%** |

SK Hynix fell with the group on Oct 30 and then **rose** on Nov 2, the session Sandisk fell a
further 6.6%. Micron and SK Hynix both sell DRAM alongside NAND. Sandisk does not — and that
absence of a second product line is the same concentration that produced +378% on the way up.

### What Sandisk itself said

Its guidance, given Oct 29, points the other way. CFO Luis Visoso said the company expects
"pricing to continue to increase modestly throughout the year", and put fiscal Q1's growth at
roughly **60% volume and 40% price**. On supply commitments:

- about **50% of fiscal 2027 bits** and about **two-thirds of fiscal 2028 bits** are under long-term
  agreements, **95% of them with investment-grade counterparties**
- bit-supply growth stays **mid-to-high teens**, with no change to the fab plan
- supply cannot answer quickly in either direction — Visoso put a wafer at "120 days or so" to
  produce

(source: Sandisk fiscal Q1 2027 earnings call, October 29, 2026.)

Contracted bits blunt a spot-price move without removing it. The two statements are not in
conflict: Sandisk is describing its own contracted book, Kioxia is describing the marginal wafer.

## Datacenter is now the business

| End market | FQ1 FY27 | share | FQ1 FY26 | share | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Edge | 5,832 | 52.9% | 1,387 | 60.1% | +320% |
| Datacenter | 4,802 | 43.5% | 269 | 11.7% | n/m |
| Consumer | 397 | 3.6% | 652 | 28.2% | −39% |

Datacenter went from $269M to $4,802M, and grew 61% sequentially on its own. Consumer, the legacy
retail-card business, fell 39% and is now under 4% of revenue.

## $513M of the profit is not operations

Of the $539M non-operating line, **$513M is a gain on equity securities**. Operating income of
$8,780M is the figure that describes the business. This is the distortion
[[alphabet-q3-fy26]] documents, at a smaller scale.

## The split and the buyback

Sandisk announced an **eight-for-one forward stock split**, record date November 9 2026, shares
issued after the close on November 19, trading split-adjusted from November 20. Every per-share
figure here is pre-split, as the company reports them.

It also repurchased about 3.2 million shares for $5.0B in the quarter, and 6.0 million — roughly 4%
of shares outstanding — over the last two quarters.

Guidance for fiscal Q2 2027 is revenue of $12,200M to $12,600M at an 84.4%–85.4% GAAP gross margin.
