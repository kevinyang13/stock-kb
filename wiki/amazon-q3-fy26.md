# Amazon Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Amazon's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $201,944M of revenue, up 12% year over year, operating income up 61% to $28,076M — and net income down 2%, because the year-ago quarter carried $10.7B of non-operating gains this one does not. AWS grew 39%.

**Sources**: Amazon 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 results, filed October 29, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000038).

**Last updated**: 2026-10-29

## Operating income is the comparable figure

Revenue grew 12% and operating income grew 61%, yet net income **fell 2%**. Nothing in the
operating business explains that. The year-ago quarter carried **$10,748M of non-operating income**;
this one carried a **$1,207M expense** — a swing of almost $12B, driven by investment gains that
are not in the current period.

Reading net income year over year here describes the investment portfolio, not Amazon. This is the
same trap [[alphabet-q3-fy26]] sets, in the opposite direction.

63% of company operating income on 23% of revenue, at a 38.7% segment margin.
