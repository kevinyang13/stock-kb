# Microsoft FQ1 FY27

**Summary**: Microsoft's fiscal first quarter 2027 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $92,279M of revenue, up 19% year over year, a 68% gross margin, $45,915M of operating income at a 50% margin and $36,629M of net income. The quarter is also the first under Microsoft's recast segments, which went from three to two.

**Sources**: Microsoft 8-K exhibit 99.1, filed October 28, 2026 (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436122/msft-ex99_1.htm). Market figures from stockanalysis.com, read 2026-10-28.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

derived for the chart.

## The segments changed

Effective fiscal 2027 Microsoft replaced Productivity & Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud and
More Personal Computing with **two** reportable segments:

| Segment | FQ1 FY27 | FQ1 FY26 | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agents and Infra | 76,795 | 61,672 | +25% |
| Devices and Consumer | 15,484 | 16,001 | −3% |
| **Total** | **92,279** | **77,673** | **+19%** |

Two bands say very little about a 19% quarter, so the Financials card draws the eight product and
service offering lines instead. They are reported in the same release, foot to the same total, and
the prior-year figures are Microsoft's own recast ones.

| Offering | FQ1 FY27 | FQ1 FY26 | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Azure | 33,165 | 22,384 | +48% |
| Microsoft 365 cloud | 27,778 | 23,694 | +17% |
| Productivity and server licensing | 8,407 | 8,678 | −3% |
| Search and advertising | 6,554 | 5,942 | +10% |
| XBOX | 5,379 | 5,508 | −2% |
| Industry solutions | 5,337 | 4,894 | +9% |
| Windows OEM and devices | 3,551 | 4,551 | −22% |
| Frontier and support services | 2,108 | 2,022 | +4% |
| **Total revenue** | **92,279** | **77,673** | **+19%** |

**Azure alone is 36% of Microsoft** and grew 48%. Microsoft Cloud revenue, the broader measure
Microsoft quotes, was $63.9B at 30% growth. Against that, Windows OEM and devices fell 22% and
XBOX fell 2% — the Devices and Consumer segment shrank.

## The other income line is OpenAI

Other income and expense was **+$38M**, essentially nothing, against **−$3,660M** a year ago. That
is not an operating swing: Microsoft's own non-GAAP strips out a $207M net gain from its OpenAI
investments this quarter and a $3.1B net loss in the year-ago quarter. On that basis net income
grew 18%, not 32%, and diluted EPS $4.89 rather than $4.92.

Both numbers are true and they describe different things. The GAAP figure is what the chart draws.

## An eight-segment chart

This is the first card on the Financials tab with more than six revenue bands, and it exposed a
layout limit. Eight segments leave about 90 pixels each in the sankey's left column, and the
standard label block — name, money, Y/Y, percent of revenue — overran it, so every segment's ratio
line landed on the next segment's name. The ratio line is dropped on this card. See
[[sankey-income-statement]] for the rule: a label carries at most two sub-lines.
