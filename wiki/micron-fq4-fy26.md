# Micron FQ4 FY26

**Summary**: Micron's fiscal Q4 2026 (quarter ended September 3, 2026) was a record: $54.23B revenue, up 379% year over year, at an 86.8% gross margin, with $37.70B of net income.

**Sources**: Micron Form 8-K, Q4 FY26 press release exhibit, filed with the SEC (source: sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm). Revenue growth cross-checked against stockanalysis.com.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-22

## Business unit revenue

Micron reports by business unit, not by DRAM/NAND, in this release (source: Micron 8-K press release).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core Data Center (CDBU) | 18,002 | 11,524 | 1,577 | +1,041% |
| Cloud Memory (CMBU) | 16,283 | 13,769 | 4,543 | +258% |
| Mobile and Client (MCBU) | 13,114 | 11,521 | 3,760 | +249% |

The four units sum to $54,223M against consolidated revenue of $54,229M. The filing does not explain the $6M difference.

## What stands out

- **Core Data Center is the swing factor.** It grew more than eleven-fold year over year and went from 14% of revenue to 33%. The data-centre units together (CDBU + CMBU) are 63% of revenue.
- **An 86.8% gross margin is extraordinary for a memory manufacturer.** Cost of goods sold was only $7.2B against $54.2B of revenue. This is a pricing-cycle number, not a durable structural one.
- **Operating expenses are tiny relative to revenue** — $3.3B, about 6% — so nearly all gross profit converts to operating profit.
- **No interest expense** in the quarter, and $575M of interest income.

## Full year and guidance

Fiscal 2026 revenue was $133.19B against $37.38B in fiscal 2025; GAAP net income was $84.97B ($74.33 per diluted share). Q1 FY27 guidance is roughly $61.5B ± $1.5B. A $0.15 quarterly dividend was declared, payable October 29, 2026.

## Context

Results were released after the close on September 30, 2026. In the weeks after, memory names sold off hard on doubts about AI capital spending — Micron fell about 15% and SanDisk about 18% in the week of October 12, before rebounding on October 19 (see [[sankey-income-statement]] for the chart build, and the site's Morning Brief tab for the daily record).
