# Intuitive Machines Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Intuitive Machines' third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $269.3M of revenue, up 414% year over year, a record 26% gross margin, a $34.2M operating loss and a $3.4M net loss. The growth is one line -- product revenue went from nothing to $215.1M -- and the small net loss is one item: a $30.0M warrant mark.

**Sources**: Intuitive Machines 8-K exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 results, filed November 12, 2026. Free cash flow and market figures are stockanalysis.com's, read November 12, 2026.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-12

## The growth is one line

Product revenue was **zero** in the same quarter a year ago and is **$215.1M** now -- 80% of the
company. The release attributes it to satellite production, and the business the company was known
for, lunar delivery, is no longer what most of the revenue is. Service revenue, which is that older
business, grew 6%.

This is why the card's split is by revenue **type** rather than by customer. The previous card
split Civil / Commercial / National security, which comes from the 10-Q; the Q3 10-Q is not filed
yet, so what is drawn is the release's own disaggregation.

Cost of revenues is stated **excluding depreciation and amortization**, which the filing shows as a
separate line and the chart draws as its own band. The 26% gross margin is therefore not comparable
with a card whose cost of revenue carries D&A -- the same caution that applies to [[iren-fq1-fy27]].

## The net loss is a warrant mark

| | $M |
|---|---|
| Operating loss | (34.2) |
| Change in fair value of warrant liabilities | 30.0 |
| Change in fair value of contingent consideration | 3.6 |
| Interest income less interest expense | (1.6) |
| Other income, net | (1.2) |
| **Net loss** | **(3.4)** |

**$30.0M of the $30.9M of non-operating income is a warrant revaluation.** Strip it and the loss is
roughly ten times larger. A warrant mark describes the share price, not the quarter's trading, and
it reverses when the share price moves the other way. The operating loss of $34.2M is the figure
that describes the business -- the same reading [[hut-8-q3-fy26]] and [[centrus-q3-fy26]] needed.

A second, smaller point: the $3.4M consolidated net loss becomes a **$4.0M loss attributable to the
Company** after $0.7M attributed to noncontrolling and redeemable noncontrolling interests. The
chart draws the consolidated figure so the statement foots; EPS is computed on the attributable one.

## What the company called a record

- record revenue of $269M
- record gross margin of 26%, which the release says includes "net favourable one-time program
  adjustments" alongside higher volume -- so part of the 26% is not a run rate
- record positive free cash flow of $33M: $66M from operations less $33M of capex
- record backlog of **$1.9B**, up $1.7B from year-end 2025

Awarded as a vendor on NASA's $10B (+$5B option) CLPS 2 IDIQ in Q4, and closed the Goonhilly Earth
Station and COMSAT acquisition in the quarter.

## Balance sheet

Debt $509.6M against cash of $463.8M -- **net debt $45.8M**, down from $114.0M a quarter ago, and
the first quarter of positive free cash flow on this card. Cash ties to the release's $463,810K.

## Guidance

Full-year 2026 revenue of **$920M-$950M** and positive adjusted EBITDA. Nine-month revenue is
$662.2M, so the guide implies $258M-$288M in Q4 -- roughly flat on this quarter.
