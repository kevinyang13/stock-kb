# Rocket Lab Q3 FY26

**Summary**: Rocket Lab's third quarter 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026): $267.8M of revenue, up 73% year over year, a 34% gross margin, a $79.2M operating loss and a $79.7M net loss. Space Systems is now 80% of the company and the faster grower; Launch Services grew 30% and its gross margin fell 23 points.

## The year-ago net loss is a trap

Net loss went from $18.3M to $79.7M, which reads like a collapse. It is not the comparison to
make. The September 2025 quarter carried a **$41.1M tax benefit**; this one carries a $3.5M tax
charge. This quarter also carries a **$27.3M loss on extinguishment of debt** that the year-ago
quarter did not.

Operating loss is the honest line, and it went from $59.0M to $79.2M — wider, on revenue that
grew 73%. This is the same caution [[iren-fq1-fy27]] carries for the opposite reason, where a
revaluation made the year-ago net line look far better than the business.

## The margin crossover

The more interesting number is underneath the split.

| Gross margin | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 |
|---|---|---|
| Space Systems | **33%** | 29% |
| Launch Services | **35%** | 58% |
| Blended | 34% | 37% |

A year ago Launch Services earned 58 cents of gross profit on the dollar and Space Systems 29.
Launch was the quality business and Space Systems the volume one. This quarter they have almost
converged, because Launch Services' cost of revenues doubled on 30% more revenue.

Blended gross margin fell three points even though the mix shifted toward the segment whose
margin improved — Launch's 23-point fall was larger than the mix benefit.
